35 years of air traffic at Toulouse-Blagnac

Traffic tripled in three decades, then Covid wiped out two thirds of it in a single year. Six years on, Toulouse has recovered less of its 2019 traffic than most large French regional airports, and the model expects 2026–2027 to stay flat.

By Elio Torga · Data: DGAC via data.gouv.fr · PostgreSQL, Python, D3

WITH islands AS (
  SELECT dest_icao, month_start,
         month_start - (ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
           PARTITION BY dest_icao ORDER BY month_start)
           * interval '1 month') AS island_key
  FROM published)
SELECT dest_icao, MIN(month_start), MAX(month_start)
FROM islands GROUP BY dest_icao, island_key;

    Data. DGAC, Trafic aérien commercial mensuel français (series APT and PAP, Licence Ouverte 2.0), January 1990 to December 2025. Passengers = departing + arriving + transit. 2026 quarterly figures and the domestic/international split come from the airport's press releases (Q1, Q2, Q3).

    Method. Raw CSV loaded untouched into a PostgreSQL staging schema, typed into a star schema, checked, analysed in SQL; forecast with a SARIMA(0,1,1)(0,1,1)12 airline model averaged with a seasonal-naive benchmark. The benchmark table is simulated from real monthly totals and used only for performance tests.
